The Story of "El Pollo Loco"

Welcome to the world of "El Pollo Loco" - a thrilling jump-and-run adventure where you take on the role of a brave character fighting through dangerous levels filled with cunning chickens and challenging obstacles.

Your mission is clear: navigate through treacherous landscapes, collect bottles and coins, and defeat enemies to reach the ultimate showdown with the fearsome Endboss.

Game Features:
• Jump over obstacles and defeat enemies by landing on them
• Collect bottles to throw at your foes
• Gather coins - collect 5 coins to unlock healing power
• Press H to heal yourself when injured
• Face the mighty Endboss in an epic final battle

Every level brings new challenges, every enemy requires strategy, and every decision counts. Are you ready to become the hero and defeat El Pollo Loco?
